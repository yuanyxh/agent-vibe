# Web 3D and Spatial Visualization

## Goal

Present understandable, interactive three-dimensional scenes smoothly on target devices, with legally sourced assets.

## Generation Principles

- Determine the device baseline and frame-rate budget before deciding scene complexity.
- Load assets by view range and priority; avoid downloading everything for the first screen.
- Provide quality tiers and capability degradation instead of a blank screen.
- Support both keyboard/mouse and touch for interaction; provide a guided tour or view reset.
- Respect the reduced-motion preference and offer alternative information.
- Record the source and license of every external model, texture, and audio asset.

## Verification

Sample frame rate and load time on the minimum supported device; disable target capabilities to verify degradation; check asset licenses item by item.
